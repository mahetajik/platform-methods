# About Me 
I am a PhD candidate and course instructor at the [Faculty of Information](https://ischool.utoronto.ca/student-profile/kaushar-mahetaji/) at the [University of Toronto](https://www.utoronto.ca/), specializing in media, technology, and culture. My research explores how [[platform|platform]] companies mediate, negotiate, and contest power in the cultural industries by integrating theoretical frameworks and methods from platform studies, information systems, and strategic management. Specifically, I evaluate how social media platform companies use hardware and software tools to manage their relationships with content creators and third parties, including partners, data intermediaries, and advertisers. More broadly, my work contemplates platform labour, platform governance, and platform power in the creator economy.

I am a 2024-2026 Junior Fellow at [Massey College](https://www.masseycollege.ca/) and a 2025-2026 Graduate Fellow at the [Schwartz Reisman Institute](https://srinstitute.utoronto.ca/). My research is funded by a Doctoral Award from the [Social Sciences and Humanities Research Council](https://www.sshrc-crsh.gc.ca/home-accueil-eng.aspx) (SSHRC).
